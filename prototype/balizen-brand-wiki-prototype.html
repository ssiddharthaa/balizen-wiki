Section 1 · The Brand

The Balizen Brand.

As the Balizen business continues to grow and evolve — activating untapped markets, reaching out to new customers, and expanding our presence online — it is essential that the Balizen brand grow with it.

We want to make sure the brand captures new audiences while keeping existing customers loyal and happy. This wiki will help you envision how the Balizen Brand is evolving, and serves as the guide for implementing the Balizen Brand Identity System — inside and outside the company.

Marketing goals

  • Activating untapped markets
  • Reaching new customers
  • Expanding online presence
  • Educating customers about our impact as a social enterpriseGlossaryBalizen trains people just starting out — handicraft, computer work, on-the-job training. "Giving people an opportunity to improve themselves."

What is a brand?

Simply put, a brand is a constellation of experiences perceived in the heart and mind of the customer. Every time a customer engages with Balizen, these experiences grow. Our brand is a promise we make to our customers — a promise we strive to fulfill at every point of interaction. Ideally, our customers' perceptions of Balizen should align with our own aspirations.

🌿 We aspire to be a company that does something positive for people and the planet — by improving lives, revering nature, and preserving the ancestral wisdom of community and artisanship.

Brand Experience.

The Balizen brand experience defines our customers' interaction with our company, its products, people, and communications. When customers interact with Balizen, we want their experience to be:

🍎 Educational about the crafts & culture

Nurturing appreciation, not taking it for granted — learning the context and story behind rich traditions and techniques. A connection of craft between cultures and time periods. Encouraging self-exploration.

✨ Exceptional value

The quality and attention to detail of the products far exceeds expectations.

👱‍♀️ Tailored, friendly-homey

Like talking to an old friend. Understanding the customer's needs, making them feel exceptionally cared for.

🎨 A cohesive, distinct design sensibility

Distinct color stories inspired by nature. Capsule-wardrobe-style modular products that mix and match — and always work together.

Experience standards

📣 Communications should always be

  • Clear, engaging, straightforward — language and visuals appropriate to the audience.
  • Friendly and welcoming — approachable, never overbearing or aggressive.
  • Real and relevant — honest, addressing what matters most to the customer.
  • Surprising and fresh — as creative as our products.

🛍️ Products & services should always be

  • ✨ High quality — exceptional, exceeding expectations.
  • 🌿 Naturally elegant — effortless, functional, multi-purpose. From holiday to home.
  • 🎁 Spreading joy — to you, your home, and your loved ones. From smile to smileCore languageBalizen's signature line. Non-negotiable — never rewritten, never replaced by "luxury" framing. Full line: "Create with love. Share with joy. Connect from smile to smile.".
  • 🌈 Delighting all the senses — textures of the clothing, smells of the vetiver, colors of the designs.
  • 💎 Unique — original and distinctive Balizen designs.

👥 Interactions with people should make customers feel we are

  • Straightforward and efficient — questions handled quickly and smoothly.
  • Respectful and confident — knowledgeable, never arrogant.
  • Great listeners, full of empathy — anticipating needs; ease for the customer, not convenience for staff.
  • Style guides — helping customers choose what's best for their needs and home.

Voice & Tone.

At Balizen, we speak the way we make — with care, warmth, and unhurried attention. Our voice carries the generosity of a family that has been doing this for over thirty years.

We don't lecture about sustainability. Our audience already lives these values — we simply invite them in. Our making philosophy applies to what we write: Create with love. Share with joy. Connect from smile to smileCore languageThe signature line. It closes reels, anchors captions, and is never paraphrased..

1 · Lead with joy, not guilt

Do"Beautiful things, made beautifully. That's the whole story."
Don't"Millions of tons of fast fashion end up in landfills. Choose better."

2 · Invite, don't sell

Do"Come in. Stay a while. Take something home that makes you smile."
Don't"Limited stock! Order now before this collection sells out forever!"

3 · Name the people, not just the process

Do"Gede carved this from reclaimed wood in his village workshop. He's been with us since 2003."
Don't"Handcrafted by skilled artisans using traditional techniques."

4 · Keep it simple and sensory

Do"Hand-stamped cotton. Sun-dried vetiver. The smell of Bali, wherever you are."
Don't"100% organic, sustainably harvested, certified vetiver root…"

5 · Grow together

Do"Thirty years of making, thanks to people like you."
Don't"We value your continued patronage."

Principles

  • Sentence and title case only — avoid all caps.
  • Generous leading and clear space; type should never feel crowded.
  • Body copy carries most of the page; display faces are used with restraint.

Section 3 · Applications

Social Media.

Conversational, warm, personal. Behind-the-scenes moments, artisan stories, the workshop in morning light. Captions short and human.

"Morning in the workshop. Ketut is finishing a new batch of capiz chimes. You can hear the ocean in them. 🐚"

Caption discipline

  • Lead with feeling and a real human detail — not a product listing.
  • Atmospheric or sensory details must be observed or reported, never invented.
  • Understated, natural copy outperforms enthusiastic selling language.
  • Close reels with the end frame systemStyle guideReel end frames: Balizen wordmark, FTF mark, or "From smile to smile" — on teal, spice, sage, or black..

Platform rules — Instagram

  • Hashtags: maximum five per post.
  • SEO: the caption carries all indexing weight — per-slide carousel captions do not index.
  • Boostable formats: carousels must be 1:1 or 4:5. Trending/library music and interactive stickers block boosting.
  • Metrics: profile visits over link clicks for brand-discovery goals.

Proven ad formulas

  • Artisan craft storytelling — named people, real workshops, specific techniques.
  • Brand personality / vibe content — the feeling of the stores and the island.
