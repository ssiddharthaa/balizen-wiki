Hashtags are a discovery tool, not decoration. Use them intentionally, keep them in the first comment to keep captions clean, and rotate them based on the content type.

**Placement strategy:** Top 5 most relevant hashtags go in the **caption** (Instagram currently limits caption hashtags to 5). The remaining 15–17 go in the **first comment**, posted immediately after publishing — Buffer automates this.

**Total target:** 18–22 hashtags per post, mixing categories below.

## Core Brand Tags (use on every post — always include 2–3)
#balizen · #fromsmiletosmile · #madeinbali · #handmadeinbali · #fairtradebali

## Fair Trade & Ethical Positioning
#fairtrade · #fairtradefederation · #fairtradefashion · #ethicalfashion · #consciousfashion · #sustainablestyle · #slowfashion · #sustainablefashion · #ethicallymade

## Craft & Making
#artisanmade · #handmadeclothing · #handmadewithlove · #handwovenwithlove · #traditionalcraft · #textileart · #handsilkscreened

## Product-Specific (rotate by post)
- Fashion & resort wear: #resortwear · #resortfashion · #slowfashionmovement · #consciouswardrobe · #islandstyle
- Homeware & tabletop: #tablescape · #tablesetting · #handwoventextiles · #homedecor · #tabletop · #entertainingathome
- Patchwork & zero waste: #patchwork · #patchworkfashion · #patchworkdress · #zerowaste · #zerowastefashion · #remnantfabric · #secondlifefabric · #oneofakind
- Vetiver: #vetiver · #naturalfragrance · #ecofriendlyhome
- Gifts: #fairtradegifts · #sustainablegifts · #ethicalgifts · #handmadegifts · #giftguide

## Location & Discovery (use 2–3)
#balishopping · #ubudshopping · #seminyakshopping · #shopbali · #balilife · #balibrand · #jakartashopping · #discoverbali

## Seasonal / Campaign Tags (campaign moments only)
#ramadan · #ramadangifts · #holidaygifts · #valentinesday · #mothersday · #christmasgifts

## Tags to Skip
#sustainable (too broad) · #ecofriendly (too broad on its own) · #fashion (too generic) · #homedecoration (bot-heavy) · #handmade (too broad, low signal) · #bali (too generic)

## Rotation Principle
Don't use the same exact set on every post. Instagram's algorithm favors variety. Keep the 2–3 core brand tags consistent, then rotate the rest based on content type. Build 3–4 template sets ("fashion post," "homeware post," "artisan story," "educational") and customize from there.
