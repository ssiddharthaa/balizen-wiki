## Logo variations

- Balizen wordmark (teal serif)
- "Since 1992" lockup with descriptors: handmade · fair trade · eco-friendly
- Teal circle badge (cream wordmark)
- Fair Trade Federation VERIFIED mark — appears alongside the wordmark on reel end frames and packaging. Language rule: we are a *verified member* — the mark speaks for the company, never for an individual product (see Correct Terms Glossary).

## Reel end frames

Wordmark, FTF VERIFIED mark, or "From smile to smile." — set on Balizen Teal, Spice, Tree Hugger, or black (white text PNGs, black background as separate shape).
