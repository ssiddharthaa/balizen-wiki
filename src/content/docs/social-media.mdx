Social sits among the dozens of touchpoints where customers meet Balizen — an ad
on Instagram, one of our stores in Bali, browsing and shopping on the website.
It is also the most casual of them, and the two need to be true at the same
time. What people find there should feel unhurried and unpolished in the right
places, and unmistakably Balizen in all of them.

## Where social sits

Social is where three things we already know about ourselves meet in public.

**Our secondary audience lives here.** The Conscious Next Generation — younger
shoppers, 25–35, Instagram-native, drawn to our extraordinary color combinations
and mix-and-match design sensibility — is reached through social media,
short-form video and creator collaborations. For them, Balizen is the Bali find
worth posting.

**Our three lenses apply, in a particular mix.** Values, the Tourist Journey and
Education colour everything we make. For this audience the emphasis leans to
Education and Values, told visually rather than explained.

**Our voice does not change here.** Conversational, warm, personal.
Behind-the-scenes moments. Artisan stories. Captions short and human. Lead with
joy, not guilt. Invite, don't sell. Name the people, not just the process.

## The elements

Everything on social is built from four elements. They are not four content
buckets to fill on rotation — they are four layers of the same post.

1. **Formats** — the shapes we shoot in, and what each one is good at.
2. **Grid & Sequencing** — how posts sit beside each other, and the story that
   arrangement tells.
3. **Storytelling** — the craftspeople, the farming, the lives behind the work.
4. **Hashtags** — how the right people find any of it.

A single post is a format, placed in a sequence, carrying a story, tagged to be
found. When one of the four is missing the post still works. When one is wrong,
it reads as someone else's brand.
