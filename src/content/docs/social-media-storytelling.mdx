Every piece we make has someone's hands in it. Storytelling on social is where
that becomes visible. It is not the part of the feed where we explain our values
— it is the part where we introduce the people who hold them. Name the people,
not just the process.

## How we're preserving and nurturing the traditions

- Letting people be at home with their family, their ceremonies, their
  traditional way of life — and still make a living.

## Organic farming impact

- Efficiency in how the work is done.
- Preservation of the environment.

## Helping those with less opportunity

- People living with disability.
- People living with illness.
- Caretakers.

:::caution[Working draft]
These three story lines are captured as headings, not yet as stories. Each one
needs the specifics — a name, a village, a workshop — before it can be
published. The frame is right; the material is still to be gathered.
:::
