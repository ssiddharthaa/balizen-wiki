**The rule: Everything is made to go together.**

The colors and patterns here are what the brand's [color](/color/) becomes in a season's line; the finished pieces are then shot to the [Photography for Website](/photography-guide/) specs.

## Thoughtful design

All products should be thoughtfully designed & aesthetically pleasing.

Examples: colors are harmonious, bags have the right size pockets that are in the right places, handles fit comfortably in the hand, sewing & other production techniques are appropriate for the use of the item.

## Colors
- Look up Pantone colors for the season
- Basic categories: warm & cool
- Fashion colors / forecast colors — incorporate them somehow into new line, but has to coordinate with existing color stories
- Color stories stay relatively consistent, but evolve over time (e.g. "Moss" is new)

## Pattern
- Sources: nature · historic (cultural; ethnic — Indonesian & Japanese) · geometric & floral
- Keep a balance between: color & reverse-color · big & small print · floral & geometric — to achieve a balanced composition

## Clothing needs to be
- Comfortable · versatile · flattering for many body types
- "natural elegance" · "effortless chic"

## Line management
- If there is a good design that's popular / selling well → do a new version / colorway
- If there's a bad design — too hard to sell or too hard to make → remove it

## Theme
- Season line campaign inspiration: must have current relevance; inspired by nature, culture (film, literature, art etc.) & Bali
- Examples raised: Film noir / Blade Runner · Tropical Goth
