A post is seen once. The grid is seen every time someone visits. It is the one
place on social where we get to compose rather than publish — where a single
image becomes part of a larger arrangement, and the arrangement itself says
something.

Grid is the second of the four elements — see [Social Media](/social-media/).
What goes into each square is decided on [Formats](/social-media-formats/).

## Thematic rows and sets

Combine an arrangement of:

- Nature, architecture, craft, art
- Bali romanticism
- Product — stylized, or just the product

## Intuitive arrangement

Tell a story with a block of 3, 6, or 9. For example, a product theme — bags,
crochet, a new colorway, a new motif.

**Bali romanticism** is the vibe in context: posts that aren't about the
product but about the feeling of vacationing in Bali. They should incite a
sense of escapism and wanting to travel — wanderlust — and are ideally a mix
of everyday Bali life scenes, iconic landmarks, textures and objects. The
Paris reference further down shows the same move made for another place.
