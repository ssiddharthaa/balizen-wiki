A post is seen once. The grid is seen every time someone visits. It is the one
place on social where we get to compose rather than publish — where a single
image becomes part of a larger arrangement, and the arrangement itself says
something.

## Thematic rows and sets

Combine an arrangement of:

- Nature, architecture, craft, art
- Bali romanticism
- Product — stylized, or just the product

## Intuitive arrangement

Tell a story with a block of 3, 6, or 9. For example, a product theme — bags,
crochet, a new colorway, a new motif.
