## 👁️ Brand Vision

States where we are going as a company and what we want to be:

**Product layer:**

The go-to source of beautifully designed, lovingly crafted, nature inspired, accessible, joyful homewares, slow fashion & gifts that make people smile - handmade in Bali and other parts of the Indonesian archipelago.

**Impact layer:**

- Preserving the traditional handicrafts for new generations
  - Training young people to appreciate, enjoy & practice not only handicrafts but all the local traditions — farming, fishing, cultural practices
- Training villagers in sustainable practices & efficiency in their work so that they can improve their lives, environment, and livelihood and preserve the environment for future generations to come.

## 🛡️ Brand Stance

Outlines our commitment to our customers:

When customers think of Balizen, they think of a company that helps people & the planet by providing beautiful, hand-crafted, and fairly priced products knowing that it was made in accordance with fairtrade values and with joy & love - all in a welcoming and homey environment.

## ❤️ Brand Promise

The single idea we want to own in our customers' minds:

**Balizen = Doing something positive for people & the planet**

## 🏷️ Brand Attributes

The characteristics we want associated with Balizen. We have gathered input from customers and we know that these are all characteristics that they believe are important and that could strongly affect their purchasing decisions.

- Fair trade
- Great customer service
- Beautifully made
- Extraordinary color combinations
- Natural elegance
- Handcrafted
- Quality products
- Love for details
- Reasonably priced
- Friendly staff
- A bit of Bali to take home with you
