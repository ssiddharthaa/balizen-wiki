Balizen sells through four web storefronts, each serving a different market
and channel. Product shots follow the
[Photography for Website](/photography-guide/) specs, and the footer's value
icons come from [Symbols](/symbols/).

## How collections are organized

Collections are organized by type of item, but also color sequenced — like the
[store display](/store-design-guide/#color-blocking).

Each collection is a named category a customer can shop directly — Resort &
Loungewear, Scrappy Kids, Jewelry, Purses & Accessories, Cosmetic, Gift & Tote
Bags, Bedding — carried across the site as a row of circular tiles.

## Product videos

Every product should have a short accompanying video on the website — Faire
supports short videos, and the [Photography for Website](/photography-guide/)
minimums already call for one video per product.

The season's own film is a separate piece of work, published here and on
YouTube — see [Video](/video/).
