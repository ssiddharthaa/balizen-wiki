Format is the first decision, and it decides more than it looks like it does. A
still asks the viewer to notice. A video asks them to stay. A behind-the-scenes
clip asks them to trust. We use all three, and we use them for different work.

Formats are the first of the four elements — see [Social Media](/social-media/)
for how they sit alongside grid, storytelling and hashtags. For product shots
destined for the website, the file specs are on
[Photography for Website](/photography-guide/).

## POV shopping video

A fan favourite. ✨ What people respond to:

- The commentary — it tells them what's important that they wouldn't necessarily know
- It's casual, light, and fun — candid
- Two friends hanging out, friendly vibes
- The way people actually talk about it: "stupid cozy" / "effortless chic"
- It has all the keywords — sustainable, locally produced, village cooperative,
  100% plant-based fibers, seaweed-based dyes, 100% feel-good purchase

## Reel end frames

Every reel closes on a brand frame. From the style guide: wordmark, FTF
VERIFIED mark, or "From smile to smile." — set on Balizen Teal, Spice, Tree
Hugger, or black (white text PNGs, black background as separate shape). The
artwork files are on [Logo](/logo/).
