## Formats that work
- Excellent photography: capture the feeling — light, fresh, natural elegance, effortless chic
- Closeups, texture — making the viewer curious about the piece and how it was crafted
- Playful use of typography, tongue-in-cheek, irony (e.g. "Our Signature Prints but Bigger")
- POV shopping video — fan favorite ✨: commentary tells them what's important that they wouldn't necessarily know; casual, light, fun, candid; two friends hanging out, friendly vibes; "stupid cozy" / "effortless chic"; has all the keywords — sustainable, locally produced, village cooperative, 100% plant-based fibers, seaweed-based dyes, 100% feel-good purchase
- Behind the scenes videos: setting up the scene (timelapse or b-roll) · photoshoot scene · setting up the stores · in the workshop — all the artisans · closeup of hands

## Thematic rows / sets (grid)
- Combine an arrangement of: nature, architecture, craft, art · Bali romanticism · product (stylized or just the product) — intuitive arrangement
- Tell a story with a block of 3, 6, or 9 — e.g. product theme (bags, crochet, new colorway, new motif)
- A sense of romanticism about Bali

## Storytelling — craftspeople stories
- How we're preserving & nurturing the traditions: let people be at home with their family, ceremony, traditional lifestyle — but make a living
- Organic farming impact — efficiency & environmental preservation
- Helping those with less opportunity: handicap · cancer · caretakers
