The Balizen logo is the teal serif wordmark. The style guide sets four
variations, each with its own job.

## Logo variations

- Balizen wordmark (teal serif)
- "Since 1992" lockup with descriptors: handmade · fair trade · eco-friendly
- Teal circle badge (cream wordmark)
- Fair Trade Federation VERIFIED mark — appears alongside the wordmark on reel
  end frames and packaging. Language rule: we are a *verified member* — the
  mark speaks for the company, never for an individual product (see the
  [Correct Terms Glossary](/correct-terms-glossary/)).
