## Design principles
- "Clean design" · color-driven layout · repeating graphic elements/motifs season to season
- Use of transparent icons
- Each season adjust colors & design slightly according to theme → open to gradual evolution

## Wholesale Email Blasts
- Content structure: special deal (a Faire requirement) · shipping info, item availability, schedule
- We share on Faire but also on Mailchimp — sometimes people don't get the Faire email
- Mailchimp follows Faire except that it includes the balizendirect link

## Retail Newsletters
- Audiences: retail membership · loyalty club members · newsletter subscribers
- Cadence: 1/month
- Add 1 or 2 specials each month: product preview "first dibs", event happening, special sale, etc.
- To add — incorporate video: talking about process, inspiration · fun, short · show smile-to-smile spirit
