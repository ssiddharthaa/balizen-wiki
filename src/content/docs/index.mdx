## What this is

The published source of truth for the Balizen brand — what the brand is, how
it speaks, how it looks, and how all of that gets applied. When you're making
something for Balizen and aren't sure how it should sound or look, the answer
lives here.

## How to use it

- **The wiki runs in two halves.** Everything up to and including the
  [Identity System](/identity-system/) describes the brand. Everything after
  applies it — the design guides
  ([product](/product-design-guide/), [store](/store-design-guide/),
  [website](/website-guide/), [photography](/photography-guide/),
  [email](/email-communication-guide/)) and [Social Media](/social-media/).
- **Dotted teal underlines are glossary terms.** Hover one for its definition;
  its link lands on the [Glossary](/glossary/), which points onward to the
  page that sets each rule.
- **The site is hands-on.** Click any image to see it enlarged. Hover the
  artwork on [Logo](/logo/) and [Symbols](/symbols/) to download it, hover a
  swatch on [Color](/color/) to copy its hex, and click the link icon beside
  any heading to copy a shareable link to that section.

## Who it's for

Everyone who makes something in Balizen's name — our branding agency partners,
collaborators, and the team members who join after us. If what you remember
and what this wiki says ever disagree, the wiki wins.
