Color blocking in store follows the [Color](/color/) palette, and the colorways set by the [Product Design Guide](/product-design-guide/).

- Should look like a house, sectioned: living room · bedroom · dining room/kitchen · kids area · gift/spa/accessories (cosmetic, drawstring bag, soaps, frames — gifting) · dressing room/closet
- Larger stores have clothing & accessories separate from homewares
- Color blocking: smaller stores keep warm/cool separate · medium store might combine two colorways on a shelf · larger stores clearly define all the colorways · colors typically follow rainbow gradient
