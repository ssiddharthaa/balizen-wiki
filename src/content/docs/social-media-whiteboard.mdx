## Thematic rows / sets (grid)
- Combine an arrangement of: nature, architecture, craft, art · Bali romanticism · product (stylized or just the product) — intuitive arrangement

- A sense of romanticism about Bali

## Storytelling — craftspeople stories
- How we're preserving & nurturing the traditions: let people be at home with their family, ceremony, traditional lifestyle — but make a living
- Organic farming impact — efficiency & environmental preservation
- Helping those with less opportunity: handicap · cancer · caretakers
