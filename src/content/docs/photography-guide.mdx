These are the file specs for product shots on the [Website](/website-guide/). For the look and feel — light, fresh, natural elegance — see [Social Media: Formats](/social-media-formats/).

## Product Photography — minimums per product
- 1 picture in setting (closeup & bigger setting)
- 1 on white background
- Should have 1 video also

:::note
On the website each product should have a short video clip. Not all products
have one yet — moving forward this should be added to all products.
:::

## File Attributes & Treatment
- Product photos have been getting larger
- Aspect ratio: 4:5 (the Instagram aspect ratio) for all clothing · square for everything else
- Color correction, clean up background
- Save as TIF with no reduced size or compression (master)
- Naming convention — product code
- Save as PNG for website: 1200×1200 for product · 1200×1500 (4:5) for clothing

## Clothing

Shot list: front · back · side · details · video

All clothing is always 4:5 — the Instagram aspect ratio, so the same photos
serve the website and social. Older clothing photos may still appear in other
aspect ratios; those are being phased out.
