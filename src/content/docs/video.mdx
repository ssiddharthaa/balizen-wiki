Every season gets a video. It is the one piece of work that reaches all the
channels at once — it lives on the websites and YouTube, and its clips become
the season's reels.

## Where it goes

- Publish every season on the [websites](/website-guide/) and YouTube.
- Clips from this video are used on social media as reels — see
  [Social Media: Formats](/social-media-formats/).

## One video per season

We should have a video for each new season. The theme of the video should be
unique to each season, and should include two or more of the following:

- Season story concept
- Design inspiration
- Behind the scenes — production, photoshoot, store set up
- Other

The season's theme comes from the [Product Design Guide](/product-design-guide/),
where the line's campaign inspiration is set.

:::note
Product videos are a separate, smaller thing: every product carries its own short
clip on the website. Those are covered under
[Website Guide → Product videos](/website-guide/#product-videos).
:::
