## Content to build

### Colour terminology — a fuller treatment

**Raised by Sid, 16 Aug 2026.** Andrea's note ("it is *color story*, not palette /
not color group") was applied to the Product Design and Store Design guides, and
`color story` is now defined in the glossary. But the terminology deserves more
than a swap of words.

What's wanted:

- **Colour stories as the larger containers** — the level at which stores and the
  website are sectioned, not just a label for a season's set of colours.
- **A dedicated breakdown on the Identity System's Colour page.** Colour is a core
  part of the design philosophy and currently gets one lede line, eight swatches
  and nothing on how the system actually works.
- The relationship between the fixed brand palette (the eight core colours), a
  season's colour story, and an individual colourway — three different things
  currently doing business under overlapping words.

Decided for now: the Identity System page keeps saying **Colour**; "colour story"
carries the seasonal/sectioning meaning. Revisit as one piece rather than
term-by-term.
